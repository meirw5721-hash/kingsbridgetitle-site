Residential

Residential title

Buying a home may be the biggest investment you make. As with every investment, you've done your research and know you've found the home for you. The house and land are tangible, visible assets, but the title to own and possess the property is not. That's where Kingsbridge Title Agency comes in.

We research the title, examine all current and previous owners and interest holders, and check for open mortgages, liens, judgments and taxes. Then we make sure your purchase is exactly what you expect, and handle the transfer of title from seller to buyer.

Our residential title services include

  • Title searches
  • Title examinations
  • Title insurance
  • Closing cost estimates
  • Escrow services
  • Residential closings
