From 2003 to a national agency

Kingsbridge Title Agency was founded in 2003. From day one, it was built on a foundation of customer loyalty and trust, and that foundation has never changed.

In 2022, Meir Wenger took over the agency and moved its headquarters to Lakewood, New Jersey. Leading by example, he set out to take Kingsbridge further than it had ever gone.

Since then, he has built Kingsbridge into a title agency operating nationally, and we've been growing ever since. What hasn't changed is the goal: to give every client the support, trust and guidance they need, and complete peace of mind at the closing table.

Whatever the assignment, you can rely on us for quality service, combining the latest in technology, superior vendors and highly competent personnel. Our experience and insight into the entire real estate and title process are always focused on you and your lender's requirements.
