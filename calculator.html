    Where the numbers come from:

    • Title premiums (owner's, loan, simultaneous issue, refinance rate), endorsements, examination, settlement and closing service letter charges: New Jersey Land Title Insurance Rating Bureau, Manual of Rates and Charges, effective November 1, 2023 (sections 3.4, 4.1, 4.2, 4.6.1, 5.1, 6.1, 6.6, 7.1, 7.2.2, 10.5, 10.6, 10.10, 10.98).
    • Realty transfer fee and graduated percent fee (sales over $1,000,000, paid by the seller): NJ Division of Taxation, Realty Transfer Fee and its fee schedules (up to $350,000, over $350,000).
    • Not included: searches, county recording fees, notice of settlement, wires and delivery. These pass-through costs are not set by either source.
